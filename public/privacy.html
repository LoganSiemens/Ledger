Privacy Policy

Last updated May 4, 2026

What Ledger is

Ledger is a single-user personal finance journal. It is intended for the private use of its operator and is not offered as a public service. Data handled by this application belongs to its operator and is not shared beyond the third-party services described below.

What data is handled

  • Manually entered records. Accounts, transactions, goals, and bills you enter by hand. These live in your browser’s localStorage on the device where you entered them. They are never transmitted to any server operated by Ledger.
  • Linked-bank access tokens. When you connect a bank through Plaid, Plaid returns a long-lived access token. That token is stored in a Supabase database operated by Ledger’s operator so that the server can fetch fresh transactions on your behalf. The token is never exposed to your browser.
  • Bank transactions and balances. When you trigger a sync, the server pulls your latest balances and transactions from Plaid and returns them to your browser, where they are stored in localStorage. They are not retained on the server.
  • Application access key. A shared secret you set in the app’s environment configuration is stored in your browser’s localStorage after you enter it once. It is sent as an Authorization header on requests to the app’s own backend.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising, no tracking pixels, no third-party analytics.
  • No selling, sharing, or licensing of data to anyone.
  • No accounts beyond the single operator. There is no sign-up.
  • Bank credentials never touch Ledger or its servers. They are submitted directly to Plaid through Plaid’s hosted form.

Third-party services

  • Plaid. Bank connectivity. Plaid is the data processor for bank credentials, account metadata, balances, and transactions. See Plaid’s privacy policy .
  • Supabase. Database hosting (PostgreSQL). Stores only the Plaid access tokens described above. See Supabase’s privacy policy .
  • Netlify. Static site hosting and serverless function execution. Standard request logs are retained for operational purposes. See Netlify’s privacy policy .

Data retention and deletion

Linked-bank access tokens are removed from Supabase the moment you disconnect a bank in Settings → Linked banks. Doing so also calls Plaid’s /item/remove endpoint to revoke the token at Plaid. Imported transactions remain on your device (so you don’t lose history) until you clear them via Settings → Clear all data or by clearing your browser’s site data.

Security

All traffic is served over HTTPS. The app’s backend is gated by a shared secret that must be present on every request; without it the backend returns 401 Unauthorized and refuses to act. Plaid access tokens are stored in a private Supabase project that uses a service-role credential held only on the server.

Children’s privacy

This application is intended for personal use by adults. It is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This document may be updated as the application evolves. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

For privacy questions related to this application, contact the operator via the application’s source repository on GitHub.
